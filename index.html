About the Project

This Erasmus+ project will run for 20 months, starting in November 2025 and continuing until June 2027. Students will collaborate online through eTwinning projects involving partners from Germany, Turkey, and Romania. The core focus of our project is exploring the integration of artificial intelligence within educational frameworks, reducing teacher workload, and personalizing student learning processes responsibly.

Project Objectives

  • Developing a collaborative AI Handbook regarding the responsible and effective use of artificial intelligence in schools.
  • Coordinating three student and teacher mobilities across partner schools to experience different educational cultures.
  • Enhancing digital competencies and fostering international educational collaborations among students and staff.
  • Raising awareness on critical modern issues including e-safety among teenagers.
